Start with a place

Destinations worth taking your time with.

Choose a bridge, a sandbar, a cave, a coastline, or a story. The route will take care of the rest.

Leyte and Samar

San Juanico Bridge

This long bridge connects Samar and Leyte across the San Juanico Strait. Come for the view, stay a moment longer to watch the water move beneath it.

Palompon, Leyte

Kalanggaman Island

A narrow white sandbar opens into bright blue water on both sides. The boat ride is part of the day; the island is the reason people lose track of time.

Basey, Samar

Sohoton Natural Bridge Park

Limestone formations, caves, and a natural bridge sit within a lush river landscape. The trip starts on the boat and stays interesting from there.

Palo, Leyte

MacArthur Landing Memorial Park

The memorial marks the return of General Douglas MacArthur and Allied forces to Leyte in 1944. It is a place to pause, not rush through.

Biri, Northern Samar

Biri Island Rock Formations

Pacific waves shaped these giant coastal rocks over time. The landscape feels raw, open, and entirely different from the quieter beaches nearby.

Southern Leyte

Sogod Bay

Known for diving, marine life, and gentle whale sharks, Sogod Bay is a reminder that some of the best parts of the region are below the surface.
