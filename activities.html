Make a day of it

Experiences that stay with you.

Go out on the water, learn the stories, eat well, or simply let the coast set the pace.

Go farther

Adventure

  • Explore Sohoton Natural Bridge Park by boat.
  • Snorkel or dive in Sogod Bay.
  • Island hop to Kalanggaman and walk the sandbar.
  • Try surfing at Calicoan Island in Guiuan.
Look closer

Culture

  • Watch Sangyaw Festival street dancing in Tacloban.
  • Learn about Waray stories and traditions.
  • Visit the old church of Guiuan.
  • See tikog weaving and colorful banig mats.
Stay for dinner

Food

  • Taste binagol, made with taro and coconut.
  • Try Tacloban's chocolate rice delicacy, moron.
  • Enjoy fresh seafood near the coast.
  • Find traditional snacks in local public markets.
Slow down

Relaxation

  • Spend a quiet afternoon by the beach.
  • Watch the sunset over the water.
  • Take a slow boat ride between islands.
  • Keep a day open for photos, coffee, and sea air.
