Your three-day field plan

Treat this as a framework, not a countdown. Travel time and boat schedules can change, so check local conditions before leaving.

Day Morning Afternoon Evening
Day 01 Arrive in Tacloban City and have breakfast. Visit MacArthur Landing Memorial Park in Palo. Try moron and binagol for dinner.
Day 02 Cross the San Juanico Bridge to Samar. Take a boat tour of Sohoton Natural Bridge Park in Basey. Rest and share your photos with jealous friends.
Day 03 Travel to Palompon, Leyte. Island hop to Kalanggaman Island and walk the sandbar. Watch the sunset and plan your next visit.
